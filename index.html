      What can it do?
      • Images: PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, BMP, ICO, SVG, AVIF, HEIC, TIFF, PSD, JPEG XL, TGA… ↔ each other, and to PDF
      • Audio: MP3, WAV, OGG, Opus, FLAC, M4A, AAC, AIFF, WMA… ↔ each other
      • Video: MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI, GIF… ↔ each other, plus audio extraction and still frames
      • Documents: TXT, Markdown, HTML, DOCX, ODT, EPUB, RTF, LaTeX, reST, Org, AsciiDoc… ↔ each other, plus PPTX from Markdown/DOCX, and PDF output
      • PDF: pages to PNG/JPG/WebP, text extraction, split into single pages, and the Combine PDFs tab
      • Data & spreadsheets: JSON, CSV, TSV, XML, YAML, XLSX, XLS, ODS, HTML
      • Archives: drop a ZIP, 7Z, RAR, TAR, GZ… and everything inside is unpacked for bulk conversion
      • Server-side (“· server” options, when enabled): Word/PowerPoint/Excel ↔ PDF with full formatting, PDF → DOCX/XLSX/PPTX, ebooks (MOBI, AZW3), Illustrator/EPS/CAD files
      • Bulk: select or drop many files, pick one target format, then download each result or everything as a ZIP.

      Everything marked without “server” runs in your browser — files are not uploaded. Big engines (FFmpeg, ImageMagick, Pandoc) download once, the first time you use them. Video and audio conversion runs on your own computer, so long videos take a while.

      Limits: the Combine PDFs tool can’t open password-protected PDFs and drops bookmarks and fillable-form data; “server” conversions send the file to a third-party conversion service.

      Most conversions run entirely in your browser.
